Based in Seoul — shooting worldwide

enp visuals is the working name of a Seoul-based filmmaker and photographer. The work sits between fashion, architecture and the everyday — shot on location, graded slowly, and left with room to breathe.

Commissions run from concept and direction through camera, edit and final grade. Stills are photographed alongside the films, so a project ends with one coherent body of images.
